# What's changed on LumiROM 8.2.5?
Only for A32

## Fixes
- Fixed accessibility

## Notes
New completely way to flash the rom.

Ok so, this post will be for explaining how A32 roms will come now.

• The ROM will be divided on 2 parts:
    - The ROM itself
    - Vendors and kernels

Why this change?
Well, the rom was too big to upload there on telegram, so I had to think how to shrink in size. One solution I found is to make all vendors and kernels on a separate flashable zip to make the ROM less in space and can upload there on Telegram.

Benefits:
With this change, there are 2 big benefits on the rom:

1. ROM will be less in size, an example of it is: ROM was 4.15GB (with vendors) and without it now is 3GB.

2. The vendors and kernel won’t need to be flashed after every update. I mean, if you already was in my ROM, you won’t need to download the zip that I include vendor and kernel as you already have it flashed. So if you rooted or changed the kernel, you won’t need to reflash it after every update.

With all that I hope y’all can understand why I made the decision.
